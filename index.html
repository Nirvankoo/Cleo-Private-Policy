Privacy Policy

Cleo: Outfit Planner
Last updated: August 20, 2026

Cleo: Outfit Planner ("Cleo", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use the Cleo mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following information.

Account Information

When you create an account or sign in using Email, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • User identifier
  • Authentication information provided by your selected sign-in provider

We use this information to create and manage your account and provide access to app features.

Outfit Preferences

Cleo may collect information that you voluntarily select within the app, including:

  • Style preferences
  • Occasion preferences
  • Season preferences
  • Skin tone selections
  • Budget preferences
  • Outfit selections and saved outfits

This information is used to generate personalized outfit recommendations and provide the functionality of the app.

Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase a Cleo Premium subscription, subscription and purchase status may be processed through Apple and our subscription management provider, RevenueCat.

We do not collect or store your full payment card information. Payments and subscription transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

App and Device Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the app. This may include:

  • App usage information
  • Device or application identifiers
  • Diagnostic information
  • Crash or performance information

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate users
  • Generate personalized outfit recommendations
  • Save outfits and preferences
  • Provide Cleo Premium subscription features
  • Verify subscription status
  • Maintain and improve the app
  • Troubleshoot technical issues
  • Protect the security and integrity of the app

3. Third-Party Services

Cleo uses trusted third-party services that may process information as necessary to provide their services.

  • Firebase

    Cleo uses Firebase services to provide authentication and backend functionality. Depending on the features used, Firebase may process information such as your email address, user identifier, authentication information, and app-related data.

  • Google Sign-In

    If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide Cleo with account information such as your email address and Google account identifier for authentication.

  • Sign in with Apple

    If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple provides Cleo with the information necessary to authenticate your account. You may choose to hide your email address using Apple's Private Relay feature.

  • RevenueCat

    Cleo uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status and purchase information. RevenueCat may process an anonymous or app-associated user identifier and subscription/purchase information necessary to provide subscription functionality. Payment card information is handled by Apple and is not stored by Cleo.

  • Apple — payment processing and subscription purchases

4. Tracking

Cleo does not currently use your information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes.

We do not currently use personalized advertising tracking.

If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, request appropriate permission.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when necessary to operate the app, including authentication, cloud services, subscription management, and payment processing.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide the Cleo service and comply with applicable legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us.

Certain information may be retained when necessary for legal, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Cleo is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
