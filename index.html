Privacy Policy

Cleo: Outfit Planner
Last updated: September 9, 2026

Cleo: Outfit Planner ("Cleo", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be processed by third-party services, and the choices available to you when you use the Cleo mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Cleo, we may collect the following information.

Account Information

When you create an account or sign in using Email, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • User identifier
  • Authentication information necessary to provide account access

We use this information to create and manage your account, authenticate you, and provide access to app features.

Outfit Preferences

Cleo may collect information that you voluntarily select or provide within the app, including:

  • Style preferences
  • Occasion preferences
  • Season preferences
  • Skin tone selections
  • Budget preferences
  • Outfit selections and saved outfits

This information is used to generate personalized outfit recommendations and provide the functionality of the app.

Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase a Cleo Premium subscription, subscription and purchase information may be processed through Apple and our subscription management provider, RevenueCat.

Cleo does not collect or store your full payment card information. Payments and subscription transactions are processed through Apple's App Store payment system.

Technical Information

Cleo does not currently use device information for advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not intentionally collect device identifiers, browsing history, or other technical information for advertising purposes.

Certain third-party services used by Cleo may process limited technical information when necessary to provide their services, maintain security, process subscriptions, or support the operation of the app.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate users
  • Generate personalized outfit recommendations
  • Save outfits and preferences
  • Provide Cleo Premium subscription features
  • Verify subscription status
  • Maintain and improve the app
  • Troubleshoot technical issues
  • Protect the security and integrity of the app
